Retention curve (day 1–180)
Model. Retention is a piecewise power curve fit to your D1/D7/D30 anchors and extrapolated to a 365-day horizon. engaged-days = Σ retention(d); LTV = engaged-days × ARPDAU; installs = UA ÷ CPI; LTV:CAC = LTV ÷ CPI (≥3 healthy, 1–3 marginal, <1 loss). Payback = days until cumulative per-install revenue ≥ CPI. Caveats: this is one *paid* cohort — organic/viral installs lower blended CAC and improve everything; whales skew real ARPDAU; the long-tail (D30+) drives most LTV, so it's the most sensitive and least-known input. These are planning estimates to stress-test, not forecasts. The scenarios are anchored to the DESIGN §13 comparables (IdleMMO ≈ 8% D30 / ARPDAU ≈ $1.08; Last War ≈ 4% D30 / ARPDAU ≈ $2.47).
